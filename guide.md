# Strategic Guide: Building the #1 Sustainability Press Agency in Africa

This guide outlines the vision for the "Review" platform, drawing inspiration from leading African economic press agencies (**Financial Afrik**, **EcoMatin**, and **Agence Ecofin**) to establish leadership in the ESG, CSR, and Sustainability space.

## 1. Benchmarking Analysis

### [Financial Afrik](https://www.financialafrik.com/) - *The Authority Model*
- **Feature**: Header News Ticker (Breaking News).
- **Feature**: Multi-column Hero Grid (Spotlighted Content).
- **Feature**: Magazine Bridge (Monthly PDF covers integrated into the digital feed).
- **Lesson**: Establish authority by blending daily digital news with monthly deep-dive reviews.

### [EcoMatin](https://ecomatin.net/) - *The Engagement Model*
- **Feature**: High-Contrast Professional UI (Pink/Red & Navy Blue).
- **Feature**: "PREMIUM" Badging (Visual scarcity).
- **Feature**: Prominent Login/Subscribe CTAs in the header.
- **Lesson**: Professionalism is non-negotiable. Use visual cues to drive subscriptions.

### [Agence Ecofin](https://www.agenceecofin.com/) - *The Sectoral Model*
- **Feature**: Vertical-Specific Navigation (Agriculture, Mines, Energy).
- **Feature**: Sectoral Newsletters (Targeted audience retention).
- **Feature**: "PRO" Analytical Service.
- **Lesson**: Dominate specifically. Users should be able to follow *only* the sectors that matter to them (e.g., just CSR, or just Green Finance).

---

## 2. Feature Gaps & Recommendations for "Review"

### A. Editorial Architecture
- [ ] **Breaking News Ticker**: Add a real-time header scroll for market updates.
- [ ] **Premium Content Layer**: Implement labels for "Exclusive" or "Premium" analysis.
- [ ] **Monthly Review Widget**: A dedicated section to download/preview the current month's publication (Financial Afrik style).

### B. User Experience (UX)
- [ ] **Hero Grid Transformation**: Replace the current carousel with a "Press Grid" (1 large, 4 small articles) for better information density.
- [ ] **Sectoral Filters**: Refine categorization to match Agence Ecofin (ESG, Green Tech, Social Governance, African Policy).

### C. Retention & Monetization
- [ ] **Newsletter Pop-ins**: Strategic triggers based on scroll depth or article read count.
- [ ] **Member Dashboard**: A sleek profile where users can save articles and manage specific sectoral alerts.

---

## 3. Aesthetic Vision
- **Typography**: Bold, serif headings for an "Established Press" feel; clean sans-serif for body text.
- **Color Palette**: Deep Charcoal (#1A1A1A), Pure White (#FFFFFF), and a dynamic accent color (e.g., Deep Emerald or Regal Gold) to signify sustainability and premium value.
